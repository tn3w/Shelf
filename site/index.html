Search

Find any book. Even in airplane mode.

The whole catalogue sits on your phone, built from Open Library. Search forgives typos, completes as you type and shows editions, ratings and genres in a tap.

Scan

Point at a cover. Done.

Hold a book in front of the camera and Shelf reads its title on device. ISBN barcodes and QR codes work too. No frame ever leaves your phone.

Track

Keep the streak alive.

Set a daily page goal and watch the week fill up. Sort books into Want to read, Reading and Finished, and pick up right where you left off.

Read

Read your own files.

Open books from any app or import them. Tune text size, spacing, margins and typeface, switch to paper or night pages, turn with volume keys.

  • EPUB
  • PDF
  • FB2
  • CBZ
  • TXT
  • HTML

Discover

Your next read, picked on your phone.

Browse genres, follow series and authors, and get recommendations computed locally from what you read. Nobody else sees your taste.

And everything else you'd expect.

Privacy

Private by design. Not by promise.

Shelf has nothing to sell, so it has no reason to know you.

  • No accounts, ads or trackers
  • No request on first launch
  • One switch cuts all network
  • Scanner runs on device, frames never stored
  • HTTPS only, no identifiers, no cookies
  • Open source under MIT

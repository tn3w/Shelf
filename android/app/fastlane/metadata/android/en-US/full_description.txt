Shelf is a private, offline book app. The whole catalogue lives on your phone, built from Open Library, the British Library and national libraries. No account, no ads, no tracking.

* Typo-tolerant search for titles, authors, series and ISBNs
* Scan a book cover or barcode to find it
* Want to read, Reading and Finished shelves, daily goal and streaks
* Explore genres, series and authors, with recommendations made on your phone
* Read EPUB, Kindle, PDF, comics, Word files and more
* Import from Goodreads and StoryGraph
* Catalogues in English, German, French, Spanish, Estonian, Swedish, Polish, Czech, Korean, Finnish, Japanese and Latvian; app also in Russian

One switch turns off all network. Your library never leaves your phone.

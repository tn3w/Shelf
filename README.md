## Why Shelf?

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 🔎 Find any book
The whole catalogue lives on your phone. Search works in airplane mode, forgives typos
and finds authors, series and ISBNs.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 📷 Scan a cover
Point your camera at a book. Shelf reads the title on your phone and finds it.
Barcodes work too.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 🔥 Keep your streak
Set a daily page goal and watch the week fill up. Sort books into
**Want to read**, **Reading** and **Finished**.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

### 📖 Read your ebooks
Open EPUB, Kindle, PDF, comics, Word files and more. Paper and night pages, e-ink mode,
page turns with volume keys.

</td>
<td valign="top">

### ✨ Discover
Browse genres, follow series and authors, get recommendations worked out on your phone.
Download free classics if you like.

</td>
<td valign="top">

### 🔒 Stay private
No account, no ads, no trackers. One switch turns off all network. Your library never
leaves your phone.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

## Switch from Goodreads or StoryGraph in a minute

| 1. Export | 2. Import | 3. Done |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Download your CSV from Goodreads or StoryGraph | Shelf › Settings › Your data › **Import CSV** | Shelves, ratings and read dates carry over |

## No cover? Shelf draws one

<p align="center">
<picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="android/design/covers-dark.jpg"><img src="android/design/covers.jpg" width="100%" alt="Eight covers drawn on device: dystopian, adventure, science fiction, romance, vintage, fantasy, mystery and spiritual"></picture>

Every book without a cover gets its own, drawn on your phone in the style of its genre.
Prefer your own picture? Tap the cover to pick one.

## Reads what you have

`EPUB` `MOBI` `AZW3` `PDF` `FB2` `CBZ` `CBR` `CB7` `DOCX` `ODT` `RTF` `HTML` `TXT` `MD`

Open a file from any app or import it into your library. Add books missing from the
catalogue as your own, with your own cover.

## Private by design

- ✅ No account, no ads, no trackers
- ✅ Nothing sent on first launch
- ✅ One switch cuts all network
- ✅ Cover scanner runs on your phone, pictures never stored
- ✅ Free ebook sources stay off until you turn them on
- ✅ Open source: anyone can check the code

## Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Is Shelf really free?</b></summary>

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription. Shelf is open source under the MIT
license.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need internet?</b></summary>

No. English comes built in. Other languages download once, then everything works
offline. Covers load online only if you allow it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which download should I pick?</b></summary>

- **F-Droid**: if you already use the F-Droid store.
- **Obtainium**: automatic updates straight from this page.
- **GitHub**: download the APK directly; Shelf tells you about updates itself.

Shelf needs Android 8 or newer.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I back up my library?</b></summary>

Yes. Shelf can save a daily backup to a folder you choose, for example a Syncthing
folder. You can also export everything as CSV, and move your library to a new phone.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it work on tablets?</b></summary>

Yes. Wide screens show the book next to the list, and the reader keeps lines
comfortable to read.
</details>

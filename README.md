## Why Shelf?

<table>
<tr>
<td width="25%" valign="top">

### 📚 Your books, offline
The whole catalogue lives on your phone. No account, no ads, nothing sent on first
launch.

</td>
<td width="25%" valign="top">

### 🎧 Listen
Play your own audiobooks with chapters, or let your phone read any ebook aloud, also
in Android Auto.

</td>
<td width="25%" valign="top">

### 📖 Read your files
EPUB, Kindle, PDF, comics, Word and more, with highlights, notes and night pages.

</td>
<td width="25%" valign="top">

### 🔎 Find any book
Search forgives typos and finds titles, authors, series and ISBNs in airplane mode.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

### 🔥 Your reading year
Daily goal, streaks, monthly stats and a yearly recap to share.

</td>
<td valign="top">

### ↪️ Switch from Goodreads
Import your shelves, ratings and read dates from a CSV.

</td>
<td valign="top">

### ✨ Discover
Recommendations, genres, series and authors, all worked out on your phone.

</td>
<td valign="top">

### 📷 Scan a cover
Point your camera at a book. Shelf recognizes it on your phone. Barcodes work too.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

## Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Is Shelf really free?</b></summary>

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription. Shelf is open source under the MIT
license.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need internet?</b></summary>

No. English comes built in. Other languages download once, then everything works
offline. Covers load online only if you allow it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which download should I pick?</b></summary>

- **F-Droid**: if you already use the F-Droid store.
- **Obtainium**: automatic updates straight from this page.
- **GitHub**: download the APK directly; Shelf tells you about updates itself.

Shelf needs Android 8 or newer.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I back up my library?</b></summary>

Yes. Shelf can save a daily backup to a folder you choose, for example a Syncthing
folder. You can also export everything as CSV, your highlights and notes as Markdown,
and move your library to a new phone.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it work on tablets?</b></summary>

Yes. Wide screens show the book next to the list, and the reader keeps lines
comfortable to read.
</details>
